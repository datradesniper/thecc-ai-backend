## 1 · What the lab is for

The lab teaches students to **narrate the map in real time** — to say what the chart is showing while it is still uncertain. Reading a completed sequence on a historical chart is a different skill from reading one that has not finished yet, and only the second one transfers to trading.

⚠️ **The lab does not require a live position, and no student is ever asked to take one to complete it.** Observation and narration satisfy every objective. Where a session runs on live capital, it runs on the student's own written plan and their own risk limits, which this course does not set and does not review.

## 3 · Session structure (90 minutes)

| Time | Phase | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | **Map** | Each student states aloud: unfilled zones above and below, equal-level shelves, session extremes. No opinions on direction. |
| 10–15 | **Written plan** | Each student writes the one sequence they will watch for, and the invalidation it would carry. Submitted before the session proper begins. |
| 15–70 | **Narration** | Students call events as they print: sweep, shift, new zone. Instructor asks "what would make that wrong?" after every call. |
| 70–80 | **Debrief** | Compare what printed against what each student wrote at minute 15. |
| 80–90 | **Journal** | Complete the worksheet; log the sequences that did and did not complete. |

💡 The value is concentrated in the minute-15 written plan. Without it, the debrief becomes a memory of what everyone "knew all along".

---

## 4 · Narration rules

1. **Name the event, not the forecast.** "Buy-side sweep on the session high" is a call. "It's going down now" is not.
2. **Every call carries an invalidation.** If a student cannot say where it would be wrong, the call is incomplete.
3. **Silence is a valid session.** If the sequence never appears, the correct output is "no sequence today".
4. **No settings changes during the session.** Log the wish, change it afterwards, note it in the discipline log.
5. **A sweep is not a turn until structure says so.** Expect to say this repeatedly.

---

## 5 · Instructor scoring

| Behaviour | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Called events accurately | Narration matched the chart |
| Stated invalidation unprompted | Every call, without being asked |
| Distinguished MSS from BOS live | Correct classification before the label rendered |
| Resisted the missing-shift trade | Named the absent MSS and stood down |
| Kept the settings panel closed | Observation |
| Journal completed honestly | Worksheet shows the pre-written plan, including the ones that failed |

A student who completes a session with **no sequences observed** and an accurate record of why has passed that session. Force nothing.

## 7 · Frequent lab moments

| Moment | Instructor response |
|---|---|
| "There's a gap, I'd buy there" | "What is your invalidation, and how far is it?" |
| "The sweep just happened, go" | "Has structure shifted? What would that look like?" |
| Student changes sensitivity mid-session | Stop the session. This is the lesson, discuss it now. |
| Zones vanish mid-lab | Drawing budget. Fix live, then explain the arithmetic. |
| Nothing has happened for 40 minutes | Correct. Say so out loud — waiting is the job. |
