Lesson 1 — What this tool is, and what it refuses to do (8 min)

Screen: clean chart, indicator off, then on. Beats: map vs signal · the four elements · no buy/sell signals, no probability · where it sits beside PEE. Say plainly: every decision after "here is a level" is the trader's. Close: what we will build across the six lessons.

Lesson 2 — Installation and first chart (7 min)

Screen: Pine Editor, paste, add, save. Beats: why saving is required for alerts · recommended symbol and session · why not Heikin Ashi · leave defaults alone for a week. On-screen text: the four default settings that matter.

Lesson 3 — Reading a zone (14 min)

Screen: zoom into one zone; annotate live. Beats: geometry · the middle-bar close · the size filter and ATR · the two volume bars · the percentage as balance not quality · midpoint · combined zones · invalidated zones. Demonstrate: switch sensitivity Extreme → Low and count what disappears. Warn: do not raise sensitivity to make a level appear.

Lesson 4 — Timeframe modes (11 min)

Screen: M1 chart, Auto → Fix M15 → Multi-Fix M5/M15/H1. Beats: levels that stop moving · timeframe tags · overlap as the highest-agreement evidence · the drawing budget and the zones-kept table. Demonstrate: the budget fault, then the fix.

Lesson 5 — Structure, liquidity and the sequence (17 min)

Screen: a completed sweep → MSS → zone sequence, then a failed one. Beats: BOS vs MSS · closes only · equal levels arrive late and why that is right · sweep definition and the naming trap · the seven-step sequence · invalidation before entry, size last. Say plainly: a sweep with no shift is one wick with a label on it.

Lesson 6 — Alerts and operating discipline (11 min)

Screen: alert dialog, both mechanisms, a live message. Beats: named conditions vs any alert() call · the message fields · prefix for webhooks · bar-close gating · the three reasons alerts do not fire · start with MSS (any) only. Close: the quiz, the cheat sheet, and the journalling habit.

Recording notes: no hype, no predictions, no profit claims. State the educational-only disclaimer in Lesson 1 and in the description of every lesson.
